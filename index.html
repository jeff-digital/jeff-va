    Case Study

    LEFMOGIV: moving a security agency off paper and onto one platform

    A brand website and personnel management platform connecting recruitment, personnel files, deployment, and equipment records—with mobile-friendly dashboards for security personnel.

    Client
    LEFMOGIV Security Agency Corp. · Calamba City, Laguna
    My role
    Website & personnel system development
    Users
    Agency administrators, applicants & guards
    Stack
    PHP · JavaScript · Firebase · Bootstrap

    Client confidentiality. I treat client data as confidential, so case studies use sample data and anonymized details.

    The Problem

    Paper records made everyday work harder

    The agency relied on paper-based applications and deployment records, had no website, and used traditional marketing. Applicant requirements, personnel files, client records, and issued equipment were managed manually, making information difficult to find and keep organized.

    • Scattered records: applicant documents, guard details, and assignments lived in separate places.
    • Unclear status: mixed-up records made it harder to check deployments, document completeness, and equipment assignments.
    • Limited visibility: applicants submitted requirements in person and had little visibility into progress.

    The Goal

    Solve agency problems and support security personnel

    Bring agency operations into one organized system while giving security personnel a clear, mobile-friendly way to monitor their own information and stay updated.

    • Establish the agency’s online presence with a brand website.
    • Digitize applications, document review, interviews, and deployment.
    • Keep personnel, client, and equipment records connected and searchable.
    • Give each guard a personal dashboard that is easy to use on a phone.

    The Users

    Two clear experiences, one connected platform

    Applicants and security guards register, complete applications, upload individual requirements, and check application status, interview schedules, and deployment information.

    Agency administrators review applications, manage personnel and clients, track issued equipment, update the public website, and prepare reports.

    The Solution

    From application to deployment, in one flow
    1. Register
    2. Apply
    3. Review & decision
    4. Schedule interview
    5. Upload documents
    6. Deploy to client

    Personal dashboard. Applicants and guards can see their status, contract, interview schedule, and deployment details. Separate document uploads help administrators see which requirements are complete or missing. The application form warns before leaving unfinished work.

    Admin dashboard. Task-based navigation groups clients, pending, approved and rejected applicants, security personnel, and equipment. Filterable charts summarize activity across date ranges.

    Personnel, clients, and equipment. Guard records connect assignments, duration, and deployment status. Client lists include year and active status; equipment records link issued firearms and uniforms to personnel.

    Reports and updates. Printable deployment-history reports, applicant status emails, editable website pages, and scheduled cleanup of rejected records support routine administration.

    Technical Decisions

    A focused stack for daily operations
    PHP, Bootstrap & JavaScriptWebsite pages and responsive interfaces.
    Firebase Auth & FirestoreAuthentication and application, personnel, and client records.
    Firebase StorageDocument uploads linked to each applicant.
    FPDF & PHPMailerDeployment-history PDFs and applicant status emails.
    ApexChartsDashboard summaries with date filters.
    Firebase scheduled functionScheduled cleanup of rejected applicant records after seven days.

    The Results

    Structured records and a clearer view of progress

    The project brings the agency’s brand online and organizes applicants, guards, clients, deployments, and equipment within a digital system. Administrators can review records by status, while security personnel have a personal dashboard for their information and updates.

    Reflection & Next Steps

    Keep the system clear and strengthen its foundations

    Task-based navigation keeps routine work easier to follow for non-technical administrators. Handling personnel documents also makes access control and data retention important parts of ongoing development.

    Planned improvements: environment-based secret management, stronger Firestore security rules, audit logs, live notifications, broader search, and support for office staff. These are future improvements, separate from the features described above.
